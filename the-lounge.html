Who we are

A small home for the sound that keeps moving.

We love house music for its openness: a bassline softening a room, a drum pattern bringing strangers together, and a record still finding new meaning years after its first spin.
The Lounge is a non-commercial listening index for house music. The minimalist mark is a record, a listening room and a play symbol in one. Artwork, portraits and previews come from a public music catalogue; full releases open in a music service.
